Our Story

An Oklahoma family helping Oklahoma families.

Built on 40+ years of occupational therapy experience and the belief that every older adult deserves to live safely at home.

Founder

Mickey Dollens

Mickey Dollens is an Oklahoma lawmaker, former high school teacher, and longtime advocate for families, seniors, and working people across the state.

He launched Fall Safe Home Solutions after years of hearing from his mother, Connie Dollens — an occupational therapist with more than 40 years of experience working with older adults — about how quickly life can change after a fall.

Today, Mickey leads a fully insured, experienced crew that brings that same advocacy into Oklahoma homes: practical, respectful work that helps seniors stay where they want to be.

Inspiration & Guide

Connie Dollens, OT

Connie Dollens has spent more than four decades as an occupational therapist working directly with aging adults. Her hands-on experience — in homes, hospitals, and rehab settings — shapes the way our team thinks about every project.

Her core principle guides our entire approach:

Every home is different. Every person’s needs are different. The best safety solutions are practical, respectful, and tailored to the individual.

That’s why we don’t sell packages. We start by listening, walk the home, and design every project around the person who lives there.
