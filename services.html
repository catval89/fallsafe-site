Where to Start

The 75-Point In-Home Safety Assessment

Our free, no-obligation assessment is the foundation of every Fall Safe project. We walk every room of your home with a checklist informed by 40+ years of occupational therapy practice, identifying:

✓

Bathroom Hazards

Slick floors, low toilets, narrow tubs, missing grab bars, poor lighting — the bathroom is where most home falls happen.

✓

Pathways & Lighting

Loose rugs, dim hallways, cluttered staircases, uneven thresholds, and outdoor entry hazards.

✓

Mobility & Access

Door widths, lever handles, kitchen reachability, and home features that may need to evolve as needs change.

What We Install

Safety & accessibility upgrades

Grab Bars

Stainless and ADA-rated grab bars anchored into wall studs or backed for secure installation in any wall type. Bathrooms, hallways, bedrooms, entryways.

Wheelchair & Walker Ramps

Modular aluminum ramps, custom wood ramps, and threshold ramps. Built to ADA slope guidelines, suitable for any Oklahoma porch or entryway.

Handrails & Railings

Indoor and outdoor handrails for porches, steps, and hallways. Continuous-grip designs for safer transitions.

Stair Lifts

Straight and curved stair lifts from leading manufacturers, professionally installed and serviced — with battery backup for power outages.

Zero-Step & Walk-In Showers

Barrier-free shower conversions with anti-slip flooring, fold-down seats, hand-held shower heads, and accessible controls.

Comfort-Height Toilets

Raised, ADA-height toilets, bidet upgrades, and toilet safety frames for greater independence in the bathroom.

Door Widening & Lever Handles

Doorways widened to 36" for walkers and wheelchairs, and arthritis-friendly lever handles throughout the home.

Smart & Motion Lighting

Motion-activated, voice-controlled, and nightlight systems for hallways, bathrooms, and stairs — key for nighttime fall prevention.

Anti-Slip & Threshold Solutions

Slip-resistant flooring, transition strips, and threshold ramps to eliminate trip hazards between rooms.

Cost Comparison

How affordable are home accessibility solutions vs. moving into assisted or senior living?

The cost of adding a lift, ramp, elevator, or modifications to the bathroom is much more affordable than moving into a nursing facility or assisted living. Below is a breakdown of the average yearly cost of a senior living facility versus the one-time purchase of an accessibility solution.

SolutionAverage Cost
Nursing Facility$91,000/yr
Assisted Living$43,000/yr
Stair Lifts$2,200–$16,000
Ramps$3,500–$5,000
Wheelchair Lifts$7,500–$12,000
Residential Elevator$30,000–$60,000
Bathroom Modifications$300–$15,000

Senior living costs are recurring annually; accessibility solutions are one-time purchases that pay for themselves within a single year of avoided facility care.
