How It Works

A simple, three-step path to a safer home

From the first phone call through the final walkthrough, you’ll work with one local team that knows Oklahoma homes and Oklahoma families.

1

75-Point Safety Assessment

We walk every room of your home and identify fall risks, accessibility gaps, lighting issues, and bathroom hazards — the places where most falls happen.

2

OT-Informed Custom Design

Guided by 40+ years of occupational therapy expertise, we design modifications around how you actually live — respectful, practical, and built to last.

3

Expert Local Installation

Our experienced, fully insured Oklahoma crew installs every upgrade with care — on time, on budget, and guaranteed.

What We Install

Safety & accessibility upgrades for every home

Every home is different. We tailor every project to your floor plan, your needs, and your budget.

Grab Bars

Professionally anchored grab bars in bathrooms, hallways, and entryways — the single most effective fall prevention upgrade.

Ramps & Railings

Custom-built entrance ramps and sturdy handrails for porches, steps, and walkways — built to code, made to blend in.

Stair Lifts

Safe, quiet stair lifts for straight or curved staircases — keeping your whole home accessible.

Zero-Step Showers

Walk-in, barrier-free showers with built-in seating, anti-slip flooring, and accessible fixtures.

Door Widening

Widened doorways for walkers and wheelchairs, plus lever-style handles that are gentle on arthritic hands.

Raised Toilets

Comfort-height toilets and bidet upgrades for safer, more comfortable use — plus toilet safety frames.

Smart Lighting

Motion-activated and voice-controlled lighting for hallways, bathrooms, and stairs — reducing nighttime fall risk.

Anti-Slip Flooring

Slip-resistant flooring solutions for kitchens, bathrooms, and entryways where moisture creates risk.

And More

Smoke detectors, kitchen modifications, threshold ramps, bedroom safety upgrades — if it makes your home safer, we install it.

About Fall Safe Home Solutions

Founded by an Oklahoma family that knows the cost of a fall

Fall Safe Home Solutions was founded by Mickey Dollens — an Oklahoma lawmaker, former teacher, and longtime advocate for working families — after years of hearing his mother, occupational therapist Connie Dollens, describe how quickly life changes after a fall.

With more than 40 years of OT experience guiding our approach, every project is built around one belief: every home, and every person, deserves a safety solution that’s practical, respectful, and genuinely tailored to them.
